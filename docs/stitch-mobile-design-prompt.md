# New Vision Athletics mobile portal design prompt

Design a cohesive, mobile-first set of responsive web screens for **New Vision Athletics**, a youth basketball training program. These are design concepts to guide our existing website implementation, not a request to invent new business rules or backend features. Use the existing homepage as the brand reference if a screenshot or URL is attached. Preserve its athletic energy, generous whitespace, basketball photography, and bold condensed display typography. Use the existing logo if supplied; otherwise use a text wordmark reading “New Vision Athletics.”

## Visual system

- Core palette from the existing homepage: charcoal `#303636`, vivid aqua `#27F5F5`, white `#FFFFFF`, off-white `#F9F9F9`, and restrained secondary teal `#4AB5B5`. Use charcoal for headers and dark surfaces, aqua for prominent actions and focus indicators, and white/off-white for readable forms and cards. Use near-black text on bright aqua. Status colors must also have text labels and sufficient contrast; avoid aqua body text on white.
- Use a condensed, heavy athletic display face for headings and a highly legible sans serif for forms and data. The homepage uses Adobe Peridot PE Variable; use it if available, otherwise choose a close accessible substitute. Keep the site recognizable without turning every admin screen into a marketing hero.
- Mobile frame around 390 × 844 px, with responsive tablet and desktop variants for dense coach pages. Respect safe areas, 16–20 px side padding, 44 px or larger tap targets, clear focus states, readable labels, and no horizontal scrolling. Stack cards and forms on mobile. Use a compact charcoal top bar with the brand and a menu or sign-out action. Parent and coach areas have distinct navigation. Keep primary actions near the relevant content and add an accessible bottom nav on mobile when useful.
- Show realistic but clearly fictional example names and USD amounts. Do not show real families, email addresses, credentials, or backend details. Include useful loading, empty, success, error, permission denied, and confirmation states. Design keyboard-friendly forms and text alternatives for icon actions.

## Screens to design now: existing flows

1. **Parent magic-link sign-in**: brand header, “Parent sign-in,” a short password-free explanation, email field, prominent “Email me a sign-in link” button, sent/check-your-email and expired-link states. The callback screen shows “Signing you in” with a retry link if the link fails. No password field.
2. **Parent home / My athletes**: greeting, account selector only if a guardian belongs to multiple accounts, cards for each athlete and current skill group, an invitation card with an “Accept access” action when appropriate, a clear tuition link, and a calm empty state for no family access. Upcoming sessions are a labeled future section until schedules are confirmed.
3. **Parent tuition and payments**: month-based tuition charge cards per athlete, total remaining balance, confirmed Venmo payment history, and a note that Emery verifies Venmo externally. No payment collection controls or false “Pay now” action. Clearly distinguish an emailed payment confirmation from account sign-in access.
4. **Coach workspace**: mobile dashboard with four tool cards—Athlete roster, Skill groups, Parent account access, Monthly tuition—and at-a-glance counts for athletes, parent accounts, active groups, and dated classes. Scheduling/attendance appears as a future placeholder until the schedule is known.
5. **Coach athlete roster**: searchable athlete list with group chips; a compact “Add individual athlete” form with display name, skill group, and group start date. Include a secondary sibling path to create a shared parent account and add multiple athletes. An account detail panel shows athletes, name corrections, group assignment and dated history. Most accounts contain one athlete; siblings may share one account.
6. **Coach skill groups**: three clearly visible current groups—Foundational, Post-Bigs, Advanced—plus active/inactive sections, add group form, and a restrained reactivate/deactivate action. Do not imply that groups have published days or times.
7. **Coach parent account access**: select an account, review athletes, approve exactly one receipt email on file, invite a guardian email for Parent Portal access, show pending invitations and guardians with access, and provide revoke controls with confirmation. Receipt email approval and guardian portal invitation are separate actions.
8. **Coach monthly tuition**: choose month, scan one row/card per athlete with tuition, paid amount, balance, and clear Paid / Unpaid / Partially paid labels. Include “Assign tuition,” audited “Correct unpaid amount” with reason, “Record verified Venmo payment,” receipt delivery status, and an internal export section. The payment flow selects one parent account, one or more of that account’s outstanding athlete charges, full total, received date/time, optional Venmo reference, verified checkbox, and a review step showing the approved recipient email. After confirmation show “Payment recorded; receipt queued” separately from “Sent to email provider” and “Needs review.” When delivery is not ready, show a paused state and disable confirmation. Use USD throughout; no currency picker.

## Future concepts, clearly marked as not yet live

9. **Coach Today / attendance**: date and group selector, session roster, large Present / Absent controls with Undo and visible save feedback. Show “Schedule not set” as the initial empty state; do not invent class times, venue, or capacity.
10. **Parent upcoming sessions and cancellation**: list dated sessions only after schedules are entered; one-session cancellation confirmation, confirmation status, and a pending makeup review state. Do not state a cancellation deadline or credit policy yet.
11. **Parent makeups and coach review**: design an empty/pending state, coach approval queue, and eligible-session selection concept. Do not claim a makeup credit is automatic or display invented availability.

## Deliverables

Produce one named, coherent screen for each item, plus important modal and empty states. Make the mobile frames the primary output and show at least one responsive desktop version of the coach roster and tuition views. Use simple, human copy. Keep all functionality above aligned with its stated current or future status. Do not add a password, credit-card checkout, pricing tiers, a public family directory, or an invented class schedule.
